# Nambiar et al. (2026)

## Paper Title
Federated Learning for Energy-Aware IoT Communication: Reducing Overhead While Preserving Privacy

## Author(s)
Manjusha Nambiar et al.

## Year
2026

## Research Problem
The study addresses communication overhead, energy consumption, and privacy challenges in IoT environments with energy-constrained devices.

## Method / Technique
The proposed Energy-Aware Federated Learning approach uses compressed model updates, adaptive client selection, and resource-aware aggregation.

## Dataset / Tools
The study uses a simulated IoT Federated Learning environment to evaluate communication and energy efficiency.

## Main Findings
The proposed approach reduces communication overhead and energy usage while maintaining comparable or improved model accuracy and faster convergence compared with conventional Federated Learning.

## Limitation
The study focuses mainly on energy and communication efficiency and does not directly adapt privacy parameters according to device conditions.

## Relevance to Proposed Research
This paper supports the proposed research by demonstrating the importance of considering device energy and communication constraints in heterogeneous IoT environments. These conditions can potentially be incorporated into an adaptive privacy mechanism.
