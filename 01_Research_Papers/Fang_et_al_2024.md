# Fang et al. (2024)

## Paper Title
Federated Learning-Based Privacy Protection Scheme for Intelligent Medical Assessment

## Author(s)
Chen Fang, Yijia Zhang, Pengyu Zhang, and Baolin Liu

## Year
2024

## Research Problem
The study investigates privacy protection in Federated Learning for intelligent medical assessment, particularly the protection of sensitive healthcare information during collaborative model training.

## Method / Technique
The study applies a privacy-preserving Federated Learning approach with an adaptive privacy mechanism. The approach focuses on protecting sensitive information and reducing privacy leakage during model training.

## Dataset / Tools
The study uses data related to intelligent medical assessment. Specific dataset and implementation details should be referred to from the full paper.

## Main Findings
The study demonstrates that privacy protection can be incorporated into Federated Learning for medical assessment while considering model performance.

## Limitation
The adaptive privacy mechanism focuses mainly on healthcare privacy and information leakage rather than heterogeneous device and resource conditions.

## Relevance to Proposed Research
This paper supports the proposed research by demonstrating the use of adaptive privacy protection in healthcare FL. It also shows that adaptive privacy can be investigated to balance privacy protection and model performance.
