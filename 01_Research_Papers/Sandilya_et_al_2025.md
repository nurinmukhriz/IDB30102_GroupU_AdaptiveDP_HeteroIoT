# Sandilya et al. (2025)

## Paper Title

Hierarchical Federated Learning for Scalable and Secure Big Data Analytics in Edge Environments

## Author(s)

Ranjit Sandilya, V. Mangaiyarkarasi, K. P. Laxmi, Zayd Balassem, A. S. Ghosh, M. E. Manaa, and A. A. Alhayaly

## Year

2025

## Research Problem

The study addresses scalability, security, communication limitations, and heterogeneous device conditions in large-scale edge computing environments.

## Method / Technique

The study proposes a Hierarchical Federated Learning framework with multiple levels of aggregation. Edge devices perform local training, intermediate nodes perform regional aggregation, and a global server performs higher-level aggregation.

## Dataset / Tools

The paper evaluates the proposed hierarchical FL approach in an edge computing environment. Specific dataset details should be taken directly from the full paper if required.

## Main Findings

The hierarchical structure is designed to improve scalability, resource efficiency, privacy, and communication efficiency compared with conventional Federated Learning.

## Limitation

The study focuses mainly on hierarchical architecture, scalability, and resource efficiency rather than adaptive Differential Privacy.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This paper is relevant because it demonstrates how hierarchical FL can address heterogeneous devices and resource constraints. These considerations are important for designing adaptive privacy mechanisms for heterogeneous IoT environments.
