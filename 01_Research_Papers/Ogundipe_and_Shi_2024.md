# Ogundipe and Shi (2024)

## Paper Title
Detecting Cyber Threats and Enhancing Security in IoT Devices: A Federated Learning Approach

## Author(s)
Motunrayo F. Ogundipe and Xiyu Shi

## Year
2024

## Research Problem
The study addresses cyber threats targeting IoT devices and the privacy and scalability limitations of centralized machine learning approaches.

## Method / Technique
The study uses Federated Learning with a feed-forward neural network for IoT cyber-threat detection. The federated approach is compared with centralized learning.

## Dataset / Tools
The study evaluates the proposed approach using IoT cyber-threat data to compare the performance of centralized and federated learning.

## Main Findings
The Federated Learning model achieved approximately 98% accuracy while providing privacy advantages, reducing the need for data transmission, and supporting scalability.

## Limitation
The study focuses mainly on IoT threat detection and does not specifically investigate adaptive Differential Privacy or privacy settings based on different IoT device conditions.

## Relevance to Proposed Research
This paper is relevant to the proposed research because it demonstrates that Federated Learning can provide effective IoT threat detection while reducing the need to transfer data to a central server. It also highlights the importance of privacy and scalability in IoT environments.
