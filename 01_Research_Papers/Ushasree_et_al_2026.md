# Ushasree et al. (2026)

## Paper Title

A Secure and Communication-Efficient Framework for Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning in Smart City IoT Systems

## Author(s)

R. Ushasree, A. S. Mohammed, R. Kumar Burila, K. Kondisetty, L. D. Panguluri, and G. Namperumal

## Year

2026

## Research Problem

The study addresses privacy, communication efficiency, and regulatory concerns in large-scale smart city IoT systems where centralized collection of sensitive data can create privacy risks.

## Method / Technique

The proposed framework uses Federated Learning to keep original data locally on IoT and edge devices while enabling decentralized model training. The framework also incorporates privacy-preserving and communication-efficient mechanisms.

## Dataset / Tools

The study evaluates the proposed framework using smart city IoT datasets and experiments with privacy-preserving federated learning.

## Main Findings

The framework aims to protect sensitive IoT data while reducing communication requirements, making it more suitable for resource-constrained smart city IoT environments.

## Limitation

The approach focuses on communication efficiency and privacy preservation rather than dynamically adapting Differential Privacy according to individual device conditions.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This paper is highly relevant because it directly addresses privacy-preserving Federated Learning in smart city IoT systems while considering communication efficiency. It supports the proposed research by showing the importance of balancing privacy protection and resource efficiency in IoT FL.
