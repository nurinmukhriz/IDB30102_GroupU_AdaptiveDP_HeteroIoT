# Khalaifat et al. (2025)

## Paper Title
Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning with Low-Cost Secure Aggregation for IoT Devices

## Author(s)
Walla Khalaifat, Jalal Khlaifat, Wael Elmedany, and Haroun Alryalat

## Year
2025

## Research Problem
The study addresses privacy and communication challenges when applying Federated Learning to resource-constrained IoT devices. Conventional secure aggregation methods may introduce significant computational and communication overhead.

## Method / Technique
The study proposes a low-cost secure aggregation approach using Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH) key exchange and masking to protect client model updates.

## Dataset / Tools
The study evaluates the proposed secure aggregation approach in an IoT Federated Learning environment.

## Main Findings
The approach provides protection for client updates while aiming to reduce the communication and computational costs associated with privacy-preserving FL.

## Limitation
The use of cryptographic mechanisms can still introduce computational overhead, and the scalability of the approach under dynamic real-world IoT conditions requires further investigation.

## Relevance to Proposed Research
This paper demonstrates an alternative cryptographic approach to privacy-preserving FL in resource-limited IoT environments. It supports the proposed research by highlighting the importance of resource efficiency when selecting privacy mechanisms.
