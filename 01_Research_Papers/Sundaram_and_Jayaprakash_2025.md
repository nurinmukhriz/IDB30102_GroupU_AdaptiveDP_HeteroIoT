# Sundaram and Jayaprakash (2025)

## Paper Title
Federated Learning Models for IoT Deployment in Smart Cities: A Comparative Study Against Centralized Learning Approaches for Security and Scalability

## Author(s)
P. Sundaram and M. Jayaprakash

## Year
2025

## Research Problem
The study investigates security and scalability challenges when deploying machine learning models in large-scale smart city IoT environments.

## Method / Technique
The study compares Federated Learning models with centralized learning approaches for IoT deployment, focusing on security and scalability.

## Dataset / Tools
Specific dataset and implementation tools should be verified from the full paper.

## Main Findings
The study evaluates Federated Learning as an alternative to centralized learning for smart city IoT applications, particularly in terms of security and scalability.

## Limitation
The study focuses on comparing Federated Learning and centralized approaches rather than developing an adaptive privacy mechanism for heterogeneous IoT devices.

## Relevance to Proposed Research
This paper supports the proposed research by highlighting the role of Federated Learning in scalable and secure IoT environments. It provides context for investigating privacy mechanisms that can operate efficiently across different IoT devices.
