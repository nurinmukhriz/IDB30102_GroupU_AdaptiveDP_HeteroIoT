# Revathy et al. (2024)

## Paper Title

Integrating Federated Learning and IoT for Privacy-Preserving Smart Healthcare Systems

## Author(s)

G. Revathy, M. Mariyammal, T. Nandhini, and S. Senthilvadivu

## Year

2024

## Research Problem

The study addresses privacy concerns caused by the large amount of sensitive health data generated by IoT devices in smart healthcare systems.

## Method / Technique

The study investigates the integration of Federated Learning with IoT to enable collaborative machine learning while keeping sensitive healthcare data on local devices.

## Dataset / Tools

Specific dataset and implementation details should be verified from the full paper.

## Main Findings

The study highlights that Federated Learning can support privacy-preserving smart healthcare by allowing decentralized devices or organizations to collaboratively train models without directly sharing raw healthcare data.

## Limitation

The study focuses on privacy-preserving smart healthcare and does not specifically investigate adaptive privacy settings for heterogeneous IoT devices.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This paper is relevant because it demonstrates the application of Federated Learning for privacy-preserving IoT healthcare systems. It supports the need to consider privacy when applying FL to sensitive IoT data.
