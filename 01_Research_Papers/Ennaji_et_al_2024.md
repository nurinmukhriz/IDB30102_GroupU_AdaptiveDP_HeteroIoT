# Ennaji et al. (2024)

## Paper Title

Federated Deep Learning Models for Intrusion Detection in IoT

## Author(s)

El Mahfoud Ennaji, Salah El Hajla, Yassine Maleh, and Soufyane Mounir

## Year

2024

## Research Problem

The study addresses the increasing cyber threats targeting IoT devices and the need for effective intrusion detection while protecting data privacy.

## Method / Technique

The study applies Federated Deep Learning to intrusion detection, allowing models to learn from decentralized IoT data without directly sharing the original data.

## Dataset / Tools

The study uses the Edge-IIoTset dataset to evaluate the proposed Federated Deep Learning model.

## Main Findings

The proposed model achieved approximately 90% accuracy in detecting attacks in the Edge-IIoTset dataset while allowing decentralized learning.

## Limitation

The study focuses on intrusion detection and does not investigate adaptive Differential Privacy according to different IoT device or resource conditions.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This paper is relevant because it demonstrates the application of Federated Learning to IoT intrusion detection while reducing the need to share raw data. It supports the proposed research by showing the importance of privacy and performance in IoT FL environments.
