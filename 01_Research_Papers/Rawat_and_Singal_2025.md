# Rawat and Singal (2025)

## Paper Title
Federated Learning with Differential Privacy in IoT Intrusion Detection

## Author(s)
Mamta Rawat and Gaurav Singal

## Year
2025

## Research Problem
The study addresses privacy and security concerns in IoT intrusion detection. Traditional centralized intrusion detection requires data to be transferred to a central location, which may expose sensitive information. The study investigates how Federated Learning can be combined with Differential Privacy to protect data while maintaining intrusion detection performance.

## Method / Technique
The study uses Federated Learning with FedAvg and Differential Privacy. Anonymisation is also applied as an additional privacy mechanism.

## Dataset / Tools
A benchmark IoT intrusion detection dataset was used to evaluate the proposed approach.

## Main Findings
The study shows that Federated Learning with Differential Privacy can provide privacy protection without sharing raw data. However, applying privacy mechanisms can affect model accuracy.

## Limitation
The privacy configuration is not adaptive to different client conditions. The privacy mechanism may also reduce model accuracy.

## Relevance to Proposed Research
This paper supports the proposed research by demonstrating the privacy-performance trade-off caused by Differential Privacy in IoT Federated Learning. It provides a basis for investigating an adaptive privacy configuration that can better balance privacy, model performance, and resource efficiency.
