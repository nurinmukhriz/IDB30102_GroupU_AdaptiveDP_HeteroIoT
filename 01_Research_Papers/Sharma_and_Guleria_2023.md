# Sharma and Guleria (2023)

## Paper Title

A Federated Learning Mechanism for Preserving Security of Sensitive Data

## Author(s)

Shagun Sharma and Kalpna Guleria

## Year

2023

## Research Problem

The study addresses the security and privacy risks associated with sharing sensitive data during machine learning processes.

## Method / Technique

The study proposes a Federated Learning mechanism that allows machine learning models to be trained collaboratively while keeping sensitive data at its original location.

## Dataset / Tools

The specific dataset and implementation tools should be verified from the full paper.

## Main Findings

The study demonstrates the potential of Federated Learning to preserve the security of sensitive data by reducing the need to transfer raw data to a central server.

## Limitation

The study focuses on general sensitive-data protection and does not specifically investigate adaptive Differential Privacy for heterogeneous IoT devices.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This paper supports the proposed research by providing a foundation for using Federated Learning to protect sensitive data. It also highlights the importance of privacy-preserving techniques in distributed learning environments.
