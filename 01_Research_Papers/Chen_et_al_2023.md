# Chen et al. (2023)

## Paper Title

Privacy-Preserving and Traceable Federated Learning for Data Sharing in Industrial IoT Applications

## Author(s)

Junbao Chen, Jingfeng Xue, Yong Wang, Lu Huang, Thar Baker, and Zhixiong Zhou

## Year

2023

## Research Problem

The study addresses privacy leakage, model tampering, and high computational and communication overhead in Federated Learning for Industrial IoT (IIoT) environments.

## Method / Technique

The proposed Privacy-Preserving and Traceable Federated Learning (PPTFL) framework uses Hierarchical Aggregation Federated Learning (HAFL) to protect model parameters. Blockchain and IPFS are also integrated to provide traceability and protect model integrity.

## Dataset / Tools

The experiments use Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10 datasets. The FL experiments use FedAvg and simulate multiple users, sub-aggregators, and an aggregator.

## Main Findings

The proposed framework improves privacy protection while reducing the communication and computation overhead associated with some existing privacy-preserving approaches. Blockchain and IPFS also provide traceability and tamper-resistant model records.

## Limitation

The approach focuses mainly on privacy, traceability, and model integrity rather than adaptive privacy according to individual device conditions.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This paper is relevant because it demonstrates how privacy protection can be designed for IIoT while considering communication and computational overhead. It supports the proposed research by highlighting the need to balance privacy and resource efficiency in heterogeneous IoT environments.
