# Ganapathy et al. (2024)

## Paper Title

A Blockchain-Based Federated Deep Learning Model for Secured Data Transmission in Healthcare IoT Networks

## Author(s)

G. Ganapathy, Sujatha Jamuna Anand, M. Jayaprakash, S. Lakshmi, V. B. Priya, and S. Pandi

## Year

2024

## Research Problem

The study addresses security and privacy concerns in healthcare IoT networks where sensitive medical and patient data is transmitted between connected devices.

## Method / Technique

The study proposes a Blockchain-enabled Federated Learning model called BFL-hIoT. Federated Deep Learning is combined with Blockchain to support secure communication, trust, and protection of healthcare IoT data.

## Dataset / Tools

The study uses a standard IoT security dataset and evaluates the proposed model for detecting normal behaviour and different types of attacks.

## Main Findings

The proposed BFL-hIoT model achieved high classification performance, including 97.16% accuracy for normal traffic and strong detection performance for attacks such as backdoors and XSS.

## Limitation

The study focuses mainly on secure healthcare IoT communication and attack detection rather than adapting privacy parameters according to different IoT device conditions.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This paper supports the proposed research by demonstrating the use of Federated Learning for privacy and security in healthcare IoT. It also shows that additional security mechanisms can introduce a need to consider system efficiency when protecting sensitive IoT data.
